## Frequently Asked Questions

### My usage time is not increasing

Check these in order.

1. **Is Screen Time turned on?** (Settings → Screen Time)
   Scroll Drain reads usage through Apple's Screen Time feature. If that feature is off, nothing can be measured.
2. **Have you selected apps to track?**
   Menu (top right) → "計測アプリを変更".
3. **Was authorization revoked?**
   Check Settings → Screen Time → Apps & Websites Activity.

Note that by design, usage is added **when a cumulative threshold is reached** (1 minute, 2 minutes, 3 minutes, and so on). It does not appear the instant you start using an app, and a delay of a few minutes is normal.

### The widget shows a different number than the app

iOS limits how often widgets may refresh, so **a widget you are only watching from the Home Screen can lag by up to about 15 minutes.** This is an Apple platform limit that the app cannot work around.

Opening the app and closing it again brings the widget in line.

### "Today" did not reset at midnight

If the calendar day changed while you never opened the app and never used a tracked app, nothing triggered the reset. Opening the app, or waiting for the next widget refresh, sets it back to zero.

### I purchased, but the widgets are still locked

Tap **"購入を復元" (Restore Purchases)** at the bottom of the purchase screen. This is what recovers the unlock after reinstalling or switching devices.

The one-time purchase is tied to your Apple ID, so **you will not be charged again.**

### I want a refund

Payment for the in-app purchase is handled by Apple, and the developer cannot process refunds. Please contact Apple directly:

<https://reportaproblem.apple.com/>

### I want to change which apps are tracked

Menu (top right) → "計測アプリを変更".

### I want to delete my data

Deleting the app from your device removes everything it stored. Nothing is held on the developer's side, so there is no deletion request to file.

### I want to exclude time spent studying or working

Use **"弁明する"** on the main screen to enter the number of minutes to subtract. It is deducted from both the cumulative and the daily figures.
